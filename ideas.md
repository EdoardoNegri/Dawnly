# Ideas

## Evening

- **To-do list at bedtime.** When the wind-down starts, prompt for a quick note of tomorrow's tasks. People who spent 5 minutes writing a to-do list fell asleep faster than people who wrote about what they'd already done (Scullin et al. 2018).
- **Breathing guide.** A slowly pulsing light to breathe along with, about 6 breaths a minute, for a few minutes before sleep.

## Night

- **Do Not Disturb.** *(Done: the Night page has it.)*
- **Wave or knock to see the time.** Without the clock always on, a hand waved over the top of the phone (proximity sensor) or a knock on the nightstand turns the screen on with the clock, which goes dark again after about 10 seconds. When the app wakes the screen itself, the lock screen doesn't flash first, as it does when the power button wakes it, and there's no button to find in the dark.
- **Always on only while charging.** Keep the clock on all night when the phone is plugged in, and fall back to showing it on wake when it's on battery.
- **Dark when face down or in a pocket.** With the clock always on, keep the screen off while the proximity sensor is covered, to save battery and stop light leaking out from under a pillow.
- **Dim further later in the night.** Lower the clock's brightness after a set time, say 1 a.m., or follow the room's light sensor so it is never too bright in a pitch-dark room.
- **Skip tomorrow's alarm from the notification.** A "Skip 7:00 alarm" button on the Night mode notification, and a Quick Settings tile to start the night or skip the alarm without opening the app.

## Morning

- **Change the sound partway through.** At a certain point the alarm could switch to a different sound, because maybe the birds don't wake you up.
- **Make turning off the alarm take some effort.** To actually stop the ringing you'd have to do something first, like solve a calculation or another small task.
- **"Get started" sub-menu.** After you wake up and tap "I'm up", show a short list of things to do to start the day well, for example drink a glass of water, open the curtains or go outside for daylight within 30 minutes, and move a little. Morning daylight is one of the best-supported ways to steady your body clock and feel more alert. *(Not built yet.)*
- **Get louder if the alarm isn't stopped.** After a few minutes of ringing, go to full volume and strong vibration. If the sunrise screen never managed to open, make sure the sound still plays, as a safety net for when the phone blocks the screen.
- **Keep the light while snoozing.** Hold the sunrise at a gentle level during a snooze instead of letting it drop, so you don't fall back into deep sleep, then carry on brightening.
- **Morning card after stopping.** Once the alarm is off, show the date, the weather and the first calendar event on the light until the phone is unlocked.
- **Follow the real sunrise.** Optionally time the sunrise to when the sun actually rises where you are, or pick its colours from a few presets such as warm candle, overcast or clear sky.
